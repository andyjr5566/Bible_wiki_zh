- 引句：Eve's conception and the birth of Cain mark the beginning of human history outside the Garden of Eden. Cain's name is significant; it sounds like the Hebrew word for "acquired" or "gotten," reflecting Eve's acknowledgment of God's role in the process of life. This event introduces the theme of sibling rivalry and sin's consequences, as Cain later becomes the first murderer. The birth of Cain also foreshadows the ongoing struggle between good and evil, a theme that runs throughout Scripture.
- 引句：The imagery of sin "crouching" suggests a predatory nature, ready to pounce and overtake an individual. This personification of sin as a lurking danger highlights its pervasive and aggressive nature. The Hebrew word used here can imply lying in wait, similar to a wild animal. This metaphor underscores the constant threat of sin and the need for vigilance. The New Testament echoes this idea in 1 Peter 5:8, where Satan is described as a roaring lion seeking someone to devour. The door symbolizes the threshold of one's life, indicating that sin is always near, waiting for an opportunity to enter.
- 引句：The name Enosh means "man" or "mortal," highlighting the human condition and the frailty of life. This naming reflects an awareness of human mortality and dependence on God. Enosh's birth signifies a new beginning and a renewed focus on spiritual matters, as his generation is noted for initiating a public worship of God.

## raw_data/kingcomments_genesis_4.txt
- 行數：79
- 引句：Eve gives her firstborn son the name “Cain”. That name means ‘obtained’ or ‘gain’. She may call him so because she believes he is the promised Seed (Gen 3:15). Soon she will learn that he is not. In the same way Samuel later also makes a mistake when he thinks that Eliab, David’s eldest brother, is God’s chosen king because this man is so tall (1Sam 16:6-7). Cain also gets a brother: Abel.
- 引句：The LORD also warns him of the consequences if he does not do so. Then sin will take full possession of him. If he listens, he will also be able to take the place of the firstborn and enjoy the blessing attached to it.
- 引句：Seth also gets a son, “Enosh”, which means “man” in the sense of “weak man” or “mortal man”. From that name appears the faith of Seth. Unlike Cain and his descendants, Seth does not expect anything from man, but everything from God. It is therefore remarkable that precisely in the days of Enosh “[men] began to call upon the name of the LORD”. While there are those who make a name for themselves on earth (verse 17), there are those who, in the awareness of their own weakness, call upon the name of the LORD (cf. Pro 18:10).
