Joyful Thanksgiving

Isaiah 12:1

In that day you will say:
Following the promised reign of the Messiah and the gathering of Israel’s scattered remnant, God’s redeemed people will respond in worship. The day of divine intervention will replace judgment and exile with restoration, security, and joy.

Isa 25:9 And in that day it will be said, “Surely this is our God;…

Isa 26:1 In that day this song will be sung in the land of Judah:…

“O LORD, I will praise You.
Israel will openly honor Yahweh as the covenant God who has fulfilled His promises. Salvation produces praise because deliverance comes from the Lord’s faithfulness rather than human strength.

Psa 9:1 For the choirmaster. To the tune of “The Death of the Son.”…

Psa 30:12 that my heart may sing Your praises and not be silent.…

Although You were angry with me,
God’s anger against Israel was righteous, arising from generations of idolatry, injustice, and refusal to trust Him. The nation’s discipline, including invasion and exile, demonstrated the seriousness of covenant rebellion.

Mic 7:9 Because I have sinned against Him, I must endure…

Psa 38:1 A Psalm of David, for remembrance. O LORD, do not rebuke me…

Your anger has turned away,
Judgment will not have the final word for God’s repentant people. Through His mercy, the Lord will forgive, restore, and receive those whom He has disciplined.

Hos 14:4 I will heal their apostasy; I will freely love them…

Psa 85:3 You withheld all Your fury; You turned from Your anger…

and You have comforted me.
The God who wounds in judgment also heals and consoles in grace. Isaiah later develops this promise of comfort in connection with forgiveness, the return of the exiles, and the saving work of the Messiah.

Isa 51:12 “I, even I, am He who comforts you. Why should you be afraid…

Isa 40:1 “Comfort, comfort My people,” says your God.

Isaiah 12:2

Surely God is my salvation;
Isaiah’s confession rises from the promised judgment of evil, restoration of Israel, and reign of the Messiah described in the preceding chapter. God Himself, not military strength or political alliances, is the certain Deliverer. His salvation includes rescue from earthly enemies and, ultimately, deliverance from sin and death through Jesus Christ, the Savior announced at His birth (Luke 2:11).

2Sa 22:47 The LORD lives, and blessed be my Rock!…

Psa 27:1 Of David. The LORD is my light and my salvation—…

Jon 2:9 But I, with the voice of thanksgiving, will sacrifice…

I will trust and not be afraid.
Israel had often faced powerful surrounding nations, especially Assyria, yet God called His people to rest in His protection rather than yield to fear. Faith does not deny danger; it relies on the Lord’s presence and promises above it. David voiced the same confidence in Psalm 56:3–4, and believers receive God’s guarding peace as they bring their concerns to Him in prayer (Philippians 4:6–7).

Psa 56:3-4 When I am afraid, I put my trust in You.…

Pro 3:5-6 Trust in the LORD with all your heart, and lean not…

Isa 41:10 Do not fear, for I am with you; do not be afraid…

For the LORD GOD is my strength and my song,
The words recall Moses’ song after the Lord divided the Red Sea and destroyed Pharaoh’s army (Exodus 15:2). The God who redeemed Israel from Egypt remains faithful to His covenant and mighty to save. He supplies strength for His people’s weakness and turns deliverance into worship, giving them reason to sing with joy rather than despair.

Exo 15:2 He is my God, and I will praise Him, my father’s God,…

Psa 28:7 The LORD is my strength and my shield; my heart trusts…

Hab 3:18-19 yet I will exult in the LORD; I will rejoice…

and He also has become my salvation.”
God’s saving work is not merely a national hope or a distant promise; it becomes the personal testimony of those who trust Him. Israel’s exodus foreshadowed the greater redemption accomplished by Christ through His death and resurrection. In Him, God delivers His people from condemnation and gives them the sure hope of eternal life.

Psa 118:21 I will give You thanks, for You have answered me…

Psa 62:2 He alone is my rock and my salvation. He is my fortress;…

Luk 1:69 He has raised up a horn of salvation for us…

Isaiah 12:3

With joy
Israel’s restoration through the Lord’s saving work will replace fear and judgment with grateful worship. Isaiah looks beyond present distress to the day when God’s people rejoice in His faithfulness and comfort.

Psa 51:12 Restore to me the joy of Your salvation,…

Hab 3:18 yet I will exult in the LORD; I will rejoice…

Psa 35:9 Then my soul will rejoice in the LORD…

you will draw water
Water was essential for life in Judah’s dry land, making the act of drawing it a fitting picture of receiving God’s sustaining grace. God’s people do not create salvation; they come in faith to receive what He freely provides.

Jhn 4:14 But whoever drinks the water I give him will never thirst.…

Jhn 7:37-39 On the last and greatest day of the feast, Jesus stood up…

Rev 21:6 And He told me, “It is done! I am the Alpha and the Omega,…

from the springs of salvation,
God’s deliverance is abundant, pure, and unfailing, like living water flowing from a never-ending source. Jesus fulfills this promise by giving eternal life to all who believe in Him, as He declared to the Samaritan woman and later proclaimed at the Feast of Tabernacles.

Rev 7:17 For the Lamb in the center of the throne…

Jer 2:13 For My people have committed two evils:……

Psa 87:7 Singers and pipers will proclaim, “All my springs of joy…”…

Isaiah 12:4

and on that day you will say:
The promised day follows the LORD’s deliverance of His remnant and the reign of the righteous King from Jesse’s line. God’s salvation will produce public thanksgiving rather than fear or silence.

Isa 25:9 And in that day it will be said, “Surely this is our God;…

Zec 14:9 On that day the LORD will become King over all the earth—…

“Give praise to the LORD;
The redeemed people will honor the LORD for turning away His anger and becoming their salvation. Worship rightly answers God’s saving work.

Psa 30:4 Sing to the LORD, O you His saints, and…

Psa 92:1 A Psalm. A song for the Sabbath day. It is good to praise…

proclaim His name!
Israel is called to make known the LORD’s holy character, faithfulness, and mighty acts. The God who saves His people is not to be treated as a private or tribal deity.

Exo 34:5 And the LORD descended in a cloud, stood with him there…

Psa 102:21 that they may proclaim the name of the LORD in Zion…

Make His works known among the peoples;
God’s deliverance of Israel will testify to every nation that He alone rules history and fulfills His covenant promises. The call anticipates the gathering of the nations to the worship of the God of Israel.

Psa 105:1 Give thanks to the LORD, call upon His name;…

Psa 96:3 Declare His glory among the nations, His wonders…

Psa 78:4 We will not hide them from their children…

declare that His name is exalted.
The LORD’s name stands above every ruler, idol, and false god. The final exaltation of God’s name is fulfilled in the reign of Jesus Christ, before whom every knee will bow and every tongue confess that He is Lord.

Psa 148:13 Let them praise the name of the LORD, for His name alone…

Isa 24:15 Therefore glorify the LORD in the east.…

Psa 34:3 Magnify the LORD with me; let us exalt His name together.

Isaiah 12:5

Sing to the LORD,
Worship rises from those who know the saving power of God. Isaiah’s song belongs to the redeemed community, echoing Israel’s praise after the exodus when Moses and the people sang for the Lord’s triumph over Pharaoh (Exodus 15). God’s deliverance calls His people not merely to private gratitude but to united, joyful proclamation.

Exo 15:1 Then Moses and the Israelites sang this song to the LORD:…

Psa 98:1 A Psalm. Sing to the LORD a new song, for He has done wonders…

Psa 96:1 Sing to the LORD a new song; sing to the LORD…

for He has done glorious things.
The Lord’s mighty works include His judgments against evil, His preservation of a faithful remnant, and His promised salvation for His people. Isaiah looked beyond Judah’s immediate dangers to the coming redemption accomplished by the Messiah. Jesus Christ fulfilled God’s saving purpose through His death and resurrection, providing deliverance from sin and death for all who trust in Him.

Psa 126:3 The LORD has done great things for us; we are filled…

Psa 40:5 Many, O LORD my God, are the wonders You have done,…

Deu 10:21 He is your praise and He is your God, who has done for you…

Let this be known in all the earth.
Israel’s worship was meant to testify to the nations that the Lord alone is God. The promise anticipates the worldwide proclamation of the gospel, through which God’s salvation is declared among every people. Those redeemed by Christ join this enduring song by making His deeds known throughout the earth.

Psa 96:3 Declare His glory among the nations, His wonders…

Psa 67:2 Your ways may be known on earth, Your salvation…

Hab 2:14 For the earth will be filled with the knowledge of the glory…

Isaiah 12:6

Cry out and sing,
Joyful praise answers the salvation celebrated throughout Isaiah 12. Israel’s worship recalls the song of Moses after the Red Sea deliverance and anticipates the universal praise commanded in Psalm 98:4. God’s saving acts are not received in silence; His redeemed people openly confess His faithfulness.

Zep 3:14 Sing for joy, O Daughter of Zion; shout aloud, O Israel!…

Psa 98:4 Make a joyful noise to the LORD, all the earth;…

Psa 100:1 A Psalm of thanksgiving. Make a joyful noise to the LORD,…

O citizen of Zion,
Zion is Jerusalem, the city chosen by God as the place associated with His dwelling and Davidic rule. Isaiah looks beyond Jerusalem’s coming judgment to the restoration of a people who belong to the Lord. The call includes the faithful remnant of Israel and points forward to God’s redeemed people gathering in His presence.

Zec 2:10 “Shout for joy and be glad, O Daughter of Zion,”…

Zec 9:9 Rejoice greatly, O Daughter of Zion! Shout in triumph…

Psa 87:5 And it will be said of Zion: “This one and that one…

for great among you
Israel’s security rests not in military strength, kings, or alliances, but in the Lord’s presence. God had promised to dwell among His people from the wilderness onward, and His nearness confirms that He will keep His covenant and accomplish His salvation.

Deu 7:21 Do not be terrified by them, for the LORD your God…

Zep 3:15 The LORD has taken away your punishment…

Psa 46:5 God is within her; she will not be moved.…

is the Holy One of Israel.”
“The Holy One of Israel” is a central title in Isaiah, declaring the Lord’s absolute holiness and His covenant commitment to redeem His people. The Holy One who dwelt among Israel is also revealed in Jesus Christ, the Holy One and righteous Savior, through whom God brings the promised redemption to its fullness.

Isa 41:14 Do not fear, O Jacob, you worm, O few men of Israel…

Isa 43:3 For I am the LORD your God, the Holy One of Israel,…

Isa 54:5 For your husband is your Maker—the LORD of Hosts…
