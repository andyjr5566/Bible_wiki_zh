Ezekiel’s Vision by the River Kebar

Ezekiel 1:1

Ezekiel was likely thirty years old when God called him to prophetic ministry. Thirty was the age at which priests began their service (Numbers 4:3), and Ezekiel, the son of a priest, received his commission when exile had prevented him from serving at the temple in Jerusalem. God met him in Babylon and appointed him to serve in a greater priestly-prophetic calling among His displaced people.

King Jehoiachin was deported to Babylon by Nebuchadnezzar in 597 BC, along with leading citizens, soldiers, craftsmen, and priests of Judah (2 Kings 24:10–16). The fifth year places Ezekiel’s opening vision in 593 BC. God’s call to Ezekiel came among a displaced people living under the judgment long warned by the prophets, yet still under the covenant care of the Lord who would promise restoration.
