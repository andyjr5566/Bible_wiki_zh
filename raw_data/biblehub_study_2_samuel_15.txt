2 Samuel 15:34

But you can thwart the counsel of Ahithophel for me
Ahithophel’s advice carried extraordinary authority in Israel and was regarded as possessing near-oracular wisdom (2 Samuel 16:23). David had already prayed that the LORD would turn Ahithophel’s counsel into foolishness (2 Samuel 15:31). Hushai’s return to Jerusalem would become God’s answer to that prayer, working through a loyal friend to frustrate the designs of the rebellion. The LORD’s protection of David did not excuse David’s earlier sin or remove the discipline announced upon his house, but it preserved the king and the covenant line from which the Messiah would come.
