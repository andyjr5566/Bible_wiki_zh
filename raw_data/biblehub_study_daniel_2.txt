Daniel 2:48

Nebuchadnezzar’s promotion of Daniel followed the revelation of the king’s dream and its meaning, knowledge that God alone could provide. Daniel did not seek personal advancement; he first gave glory to the God of heaven, and God used his faithfulness to place him where he could serve in a pagan empire. His rise recalls Joseph’s elevation under Pharaoh after God revealed dreams through him (Genesis 41:39–41).
