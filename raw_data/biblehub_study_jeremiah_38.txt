Jeremiah 38:1

Now Shephatiah son of Mattan, Gedaliah son of Pashhur, Jucal son of Shelemiah, and Pashhur son of Malchijah
These individuals were officials in the court of King Zedekiah of Judah. Their lineage indicates their roles and influence within the political and religious hierarchy of Jerusalem. Shephatiah, Gedaliah, Jucal, and Pashhur were likely part of the ruling elite, possibly priests or royal advisors, given their familial connections. The mention of their fathers’ names underscores the importance of lineage and heritage in ancient Israelite society, where family ties often determined one’s social and political standing. This group of officials was opposed to Jeremiah’s prophecies, which they perceived as demoralizing and treasonous during a time of national crisis.

The officials regarded such preaching as treason and a threat to public resolve, but Jeremiah’s faithfulness required him to speak God’s truth regardless of the cost.
