Jeremiah 52:6

By the ninth day of the fourth month,
This phrase refers to a specific time during the siege of Jerusalem by the Babylonians, which began in the tenth month of the ninth year of King Zedekiah’s reign. The fourth month corresponds to Tammuz in the Hebrew calendar, roughly June-July. The ninth day marks a significant point in the siege, highlighting the prolonged suffering of the city. This timing is crucial as it fulfills the prophecy of Jeremiah regarding the fall of Jerusalem (Jeremiah 34:2-3).
