Ezekiel 12:1

Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying,
Ezekiel spoke by divine commission during Judah’s exile in Babylon. The message that follows addresses a rebellious people who had seen God’s judgments yet refused to heed His warnings. God would command Ezekiel to portray the coming flight and capture of Jerusalem’s leaders, including King Zedekiah, showing that the remaining inhabitants of Judah would also go into captivity. The LORD had not abandoned His covenant authority; He continued to warn, judge, and preserve a remnant for His name. Ezekiel’s reception of God’s word also anticipates the fuller revelation given in Jesus Christ, through whom God has finally spoken and made His will known.
