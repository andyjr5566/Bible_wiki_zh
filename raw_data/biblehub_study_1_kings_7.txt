He built the House of the Forest of Lebanon
Solomon added this great cedar-lined hall to the royal complex in Jerusalem. Its forest-like rows of pillars likely gave the building its name, while the cedar imported from Lebanon displayed the wealth and international reach of Solomon’s kingdom. It later housed royal shields and functioned as part of the palace’s armory and ceremonial center (1 Kings 10:16–17). The splendor of the royal house testified to Israel’s prosperity, yet it stood beneath the greater significance of the temple, where the Lord had placed His name.
