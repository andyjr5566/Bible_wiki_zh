A Prophecy to the Mountains of Israel

Ezekiel 36:1

“And you, son of man,
God addresses Ezekiel by the title used repeatedly throughout the book, reminding the prophet of his human weakness before the sovereign Lord. Ezekiel stands as God’s appointed messenger to a rebellious people, bearing words of judgment and later words of restoration. Jesus also called Himself the Son of Man, identifying Himself with humanity while revealing His authority to fulfill God’s promised kingdom.

prophesy to the mountains of Israel
The mountains represent the actual land God gave to Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and their descendants. Having witnessed Israel’s idolatry, invasion, and desolation, the land would now hear a promise of renewal. Ezekiel 6 announced judgment against Israel’s mountains because of pagan worship; Ezekiel 36 turns toward restoration after judgment has accomplished its purpose.

Israel’s hills, fields, cities, and valleys were bound to the covenant promises of God. Foreign nations had treated the land as a spoil of war and had mocked Israel’s God by claiming its inheritance. God’s coming restoration would show that He had not abandoned either His people or the land He promised them.

The Lord’s word governs nations, peoples, and creation itself. The command recalls Israel’s call to hear and obey the Lord in covenant faithfulness. What God declares concerning Israel’s restoration will surely come to pass, because His purpose rests on His own holy name and unfailing faithfulness.
