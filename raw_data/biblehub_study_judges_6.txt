and the hand of Midian prevailed against Israel.
Midian, descended from Abraham through Keturah (Genesis 25:1–2), had become a powerful enemy of Israel. For seven years the Lord allowed their domination because Israel had turned again to Baal worship and abandoned covenant faithfulness. Midianite raiders, often joined by Amalekites and eastern peoples, invaded at harvesttime, stripping the land of crops and livestock (Judges 6:3–6). Israel’s strength, land, and security could not preserve them when they persisted in rebellion. Their oppression follows the recurring pattern in Judges: sin brought servitude, and servitude would eventually drive the people to seek the Lord.
