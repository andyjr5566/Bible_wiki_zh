The Descendants of Naphtali

The sons of Naphtali:
Naphtali, Jacob’s sixth son and Bilhah’s second, became the father of a tribe settled in northern Canaan near the Sea of Galilee. Its territory included important sites such as Kedesh and Hazor and lay along major routes often threatened by northern invaders. Isaiah foretold that the land of Naphtali, once humbled by oppression, would see a great light; Matthew identifies that light with the beginning of Jesus’ public ministry in Galilee.

Parallel genealogies record the same ancestral house, sometimes with variant spellings, reflecting the transmission of names through generations. Their inclusion affirms that God’s covenant purposes embraced every tribe and household of Israel.
