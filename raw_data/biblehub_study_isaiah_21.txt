The burden, a prophetic oracle often of judgment, announces that God’s judgment reaches even nations beyond Israel’s borders. Dumah, meaning “silence” or “stillness,” fittingly points to the desolation that follows divine judgment upon proud and hostile nations. Edom had repeatedly opposed Israel and rejoiced over Jerusalem’s distress, inviting the judgments later declared in Obadiah and Ezekiel.
