The man clothed in linen is often interpreted as an angelic figure or a divine messenger. Linen garments were typically worn by priests, symbolizing purity and holiness (Exodus 28:39-42). This attire signifies the sacred nature of his mission. In the broader context of Ezekiel, this figure represents God’s authority and the execution of His will.
