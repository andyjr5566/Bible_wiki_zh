1 Chronicles 18:15

Joab son of Zeruiah was over the army;
Joab, David’s nephew through his sister Zeruiah, commanded Israel’s forces during the years in which David secured and extended the kingdom. He distinguished himself in the capture of Jerusalem and led campaigns against surrounding nations. Yet his military ability was joined to a dangerous independence. Joab murdered Abner in revenge for Asahel, killed Absalom contrary to David’s command, and later supported Adonijah’s attempt to seize the throne. His career shows how God used a powerful and flawed man to establish David’s kingdom, while Joab’s final judgment under Solomon showed that violence and disloyalty would not escape God’s justice.
