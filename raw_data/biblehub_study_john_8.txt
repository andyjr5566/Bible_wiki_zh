The scribes were trained interpreters of the Law, and the Pharisees were influential religious leaders known for strict observance of both the Law of Moses and their inherited traditions. Their arrival interrupts Jesus’ public teaching in the temple courts and reflects their continuing efforts to challenge His authority. Adultery carried the death penalty under the Law of Moses, yet Leviticus 20:10 and Deuteronomy 22:22 required judgment upon both guilty parties. The absence of the man exposes the injustice of the accusation and suggests that the woman had been made an instrument in a planned attempt to ensnare Jesus.
