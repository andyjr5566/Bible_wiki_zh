John 20:30

Jesus’ works were far more numerous than the selected signs recorded by John. Each sign revealed His authority as the eternal Son: over creation, sickness, demons, hunger, nature, blindness, death, and the grave. The recorded signs point beyond the miracles themselves to Jesus as the promised Messiah and the Giver of life.

The disciples witnessed Jesus’ ministry, death, resurrection, and appearances after rising from the dead. Their testimony rested on what they had seen and heard, fulfilling the biblical pattern of confirmed witness. Their firsthand knowledge equipped them to proclaim without uncertainty that Jesus had conquered death.

John did not intend to provide a complete record of Jesus’ earthly ministry. He selected events that clearly display Christ’s identity and call readers to faith.
