Saul Tries to Kill David

1 Samuel 19:1

Then Saul ordered his son Jonathan and all his servants to kill David.
Saul’s hostility toward David has grown from private attempts on David’s life to an open command issued within the royal court. Fear, jealousy, and resistance to God’s judgment drive Saul to pursue the man whom God has chosen to succeed him. By involving Jonathan and all his servants, Saul seeks to turn the entire household against David and makes his personal resentment a matter of state policy. The command also exposes the spiritual disorder in Saul’s reign: the king entrusted to shepherd Israel now plots against Israel’s faithful defender.
