Benaiah succeeded Joab as commander after Solomon removed Joab for his earlier murders and his support of Adonijah’s attempted seizure of the throne (1 Kings 2:28–35). Already renowned among David’s mighty men for extraordinary courage and decisive action, Benaiah had remained loyal to David and then helped secure Solomon’s rightful accession (2 Samuel 23:20–23; 1 Kings 1:32–40). His appointment placed a proven servant at the head of Israel’s military during the establishment of Solomon’s kingdom. The transfer of command from Joab to Benaiah showed that royal authority and national security were to rest in the hands of those faithful to God’s appointed king.
