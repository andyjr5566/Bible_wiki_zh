The unnamed month does not lessen the precision of God’s timing. While Judah’s strength was failing and the nations watched its downfall, the Lord addressed Tyre’s response to Jerusalem’s ruin.

The specificity of the date underscores the importance of the message. The precision of the date also highlights the prophetic nature of Ezekiel’s message, emphasizing that it is a direct revelation from God.
