Jeremiah 5:20

God commands Jeremiah to address the covenant people as a whole. Jacob, renamed Israel by God, was the father of the twelve tribes, and his household represents the nation descended from him. Their shared ancestry carried with it the responsibilities of God’s covenant, including exclusive worship, obedience to His law, and justice toward one another. Though Israel had repeatedly strayed into idolatry and rebellion, the Lord had not forgotten His covenant purposes or withheld His warning.

Jeremiah’s public proclamation confronted a people who retained religious forms while resisting God’s rule. The message called Judah to recognize its sin, repent, and return to the Lord before the judgment that had overtaken Israel came upon Jerusalem as well.
