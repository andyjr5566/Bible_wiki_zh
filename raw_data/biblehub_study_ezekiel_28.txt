A Prophecy against the Ruler of Tyre

Ezekiel 28:1

And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,
God entrusted Ezekiel with an authoritative message against the prince of Tyre, a ruler whose wealth, influence, and pride had led him to exalt himself as a god. From Babylonian exile, Ezekiel announced that the Lord remained sovereign over Israel, the surrounding nations, and every ruler who claimed glory belonging to God alone. The judgment of Tyre would reveal that no political power, commercial success, or human wisdom can shield the proud from the righteous rule of the Lord.

Tyre’s ruler stood under God’s judgment, yet the exalted imagery that follows also reveals the deeper spiritual character of rebellion against God, recalling Satan’s fall through pride and self-exaltation (Isaiah 14:12–15). The Lord remains sovereign over Israel, the nations, and every ruler who sets himself against His authority.
