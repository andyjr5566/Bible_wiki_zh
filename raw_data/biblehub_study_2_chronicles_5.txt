The Ark Enters the Temple

2 Chronicles 5:1

So all the work that Solomon had performed for the house of the LORD was completed.
Solomon completed the Temple after seven years of construction, fulfilling the Lord’s promise that David’s son would build a house for His name. The permanent sanctuary replaced the portable tabernacle as Israel’s appointed center of sacrifice, prayer, and covenant worship. Yet the Temple did not confine God, whom heaven itself cannot contain; it testified that the holy God had graciously placed His name among His people. The completed work also displayed the faithfulness of God across generations, joining David’s preparation to Solomon’s obedience.

The occasion joined Israel’s leaders, priests, Levites, and people in acknowledging that the Lord alone was Israel’s true King.
