Elijah and Moses stand as eminent witnesses to God’s revelation in the Old Testament. Moses received the Law and led Israel from Egypt; Elijah called Israel back to covenant faithfulness and was taken into heaven without dying. Both encountered God’s glory on mountains, Moses at Sinai and Elijah at Horeb. Their recognizable presence confirms personal identity beyond death and anticipates the resurrection hope. Together they testify that the Law and the Prophets find their fulfillment in Jesus.
