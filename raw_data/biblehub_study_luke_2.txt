and in favor with God
The Father’s pleasure rested upon the Son throughout His earthly obedience. Like Samuel, Jesus grew before the Lord, but He alone lived in perfect holiness and fulfilled every command of God. At His baptism, the Father openly declared, “This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased” (Matthew 3:17). His life of prayer, obedience, and devotion manifested the fellowship He eternally shared with the Father.

Jesus lived among people with humility, truthfulness, compassion, and integrity. Those who heard Him recognized a wisdom beyond ordinary human ability, even when many later rejected His claims. His favor with people anticipated the ministry through which He would draw disciples, minister to the needy, confront hypocrisy, and reveal God’s grace to Israel and the nations.
