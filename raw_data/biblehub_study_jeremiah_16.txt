Disaster Predicted

Jeremiah 16:1

Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying,
God again addressed Jeremiah directly, confirming his calling as the Lord’s appointed messenger to Judah. The message that follows required Jeremiah to embody God’s warning through his own life, demonstrating that prophetic ministry involved both proclamation and personal obedience. Jeremiah’s reception of God’s word continued the pattern established at his calling, when the Lord appointed him “a prophet to the nations” (Jeremiah 1:4–10). The authority of his warnings rested not in personal opinion or political insight but in the revealed word of the LORD. God’s self-disclosure through the prophets prepared the way for His fullest revelation in Jesus Christ, the eternal Word who became flesh (John 1:14).
