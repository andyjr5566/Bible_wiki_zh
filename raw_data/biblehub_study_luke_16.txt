The Law and the Prophets

Luke 16:14

The Pharisees, who were lovers of money,
The Pharisees were influential teachers and interpreters of the Law who stressed careful obedience to Scripture and ancestral traditions. Yet their devotion to wealth exposed a divided allegiance. Jesus had just taught that no servant can serve both God and money (Luke 16:13). Their desire for financial gain contradicted the Law’s concern for justice, mercy, generosity toward the poor, and wholehearted love for God. Jesus later condemned religious leaders who neglected the weightier matters of the Law while maintaining outward religious observance (Matthew 23:23).
