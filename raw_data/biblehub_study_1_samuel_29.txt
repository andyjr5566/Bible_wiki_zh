Aphek served as a strategic gathering place for Philistine campaigns against Israel. The assembling of all the Philistine forces reveals a major, unified invasion intended to settle the struggle for control of the land. Aphek had earlier been associated with Israel’s defeat and the capture of the ark of God (1 Samuel 4), recalling the seriousness of Israel’s conflict with this persistent enemy. David was traveling with Achish and the Philistine army at this moment, placing him in a painful position between his refuge among the Philistines and his covenant loyalty to Israel.
