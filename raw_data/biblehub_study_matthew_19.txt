The Pharisees approached Jesus not as humble seekers of God’s will, but to ensnare Him in a public controversy. Their challenge arose in the region beyond the Jordan, under Herod Antipas, whose unlawful marriage to Herodias had led to John the Baptist’s imprisonment and death. “Is it lawful for a man to divorce his wife for any reason?”
The question centers on Deuteronomy 24:1–4 and the competing interpretations of its allowance for a certificate of divorce. Some teachers restricted divorce to grave marital unfaithfulness, while others permitted a husband to dismiss his wife for minor dissatisfaction. Such practices left women especially vulnerable in a society where husbands held legal power over the marriage bond. The Pharisees sought a ruling on the limits of divorce, but Jesus would reveal that Moses regulated human hardness of heart rather than defining God’s original intention. Marriage is a covenantal union formed by God, calling husband and wife to faithfulness, purity, and lifelong devotion.
