Haman had used his exalted position to secure a decree for the destruction of the Jews (Esther 3:8–13), while the king had approved it without understanding its full implications. Their attendance at Esther’s banquet brought the enemy of God’s people into the very setting where his scheme would be exposed.

Haman had offered the king ten thousand talents of silver in connection with his plan (Esther 3:9), and the royal edict commanded that every Jew be destroyed, killed, and annihilated on a single day (Esther 3:13). Esther’s position as queen could not separate her from the fate appointed for her people.

Esther’s banquet provided the setting for her courageous appeal, and the king’s attention showed the favor she had received in his presence.
