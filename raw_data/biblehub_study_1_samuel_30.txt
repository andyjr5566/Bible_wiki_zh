Their raid continued the long hostility of Amalek toward Israel, remembered from their attack on the weak and straggling Israelites after the exodus (Exodus 17:8–16; Deuteronomy 25:17–19). Captives were valuable as slaves, property, or subjects for ransom, so the invaders carried away the people as part of their plunder.
