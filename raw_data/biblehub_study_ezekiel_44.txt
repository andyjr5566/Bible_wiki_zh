so that a blessing may rest upon your homes.
God attached His covenant favor to a household that honored Him in worship and daily life. The promised blessing was not a payment for generosity but the gracious fruit of living under God’s rule. As Israel gave the Lord His due through the priests, their homes were to become places marked by His provision, protection, and peace.

Ezekiel 44:31

The priests may not eat any bird or animal found dead or torn by wild beasts.
Animals that died naturally or were killed by predators had not been properly drained of blood and could communicate ceremonial uncleanness. Leviticus 17:15 and 22:8 established this prohibition, particularly guarding the purity of those who drew near to God in priestly service. Death, decay, and blood outside their appointed sacrificial use reminded Israel of sin’s curse and of humanity’s need for cleansing.
