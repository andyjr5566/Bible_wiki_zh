Questions about Fasting

Mark 2:18

Now John’s disciples and the Pharisees were often fasting.
John’s disciples practiced fasting in keeping with his call to repentance and readiness for the Messiah. The Pharisees also observed regular fasts, often beyond the fasting required on the Day of Atonement, as public demonstrations of religious devotion.
