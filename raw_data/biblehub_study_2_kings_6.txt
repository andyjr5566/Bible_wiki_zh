The king rightly understood that the siege and famine had occurred under God’s sovereign judgment. Israel’s idolatry and persistent rebellion had brought covenant discipline, but Jehoram treated the Lord as the source of his misery without acknowledging the nation’s guilt.

Why should I wait for the LORD any longer?”
Jehoram’s words reveal unbelief under pressure. Rather than seeking God’s mercy and submitting to His timing, he was ready to abandon hope in the Lord and silence the prophet. God would answer this despair immediately through Elisha’s promise of sudden deliverance, proving that the Lord’s help was not delayed beyond His appointed time.
