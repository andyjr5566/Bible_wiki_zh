Jabin, king of Canaan at Hazor, possessed a military force that appeared overwhelming to the largely infantry-based tribes of Israel. Sisera’s flight on foot reveals the completeness of Israel’s victory. The commander of Jabin’s chariots had abandoned the instrument of his military strength and fled alone for his life. Heber belonged to the Kenites, a people connected with Moses’ family and long associated with Israel, though Heber had separated from the larger Kenite settlement and lived near Kedesh. Sisera sought Jael’s tent expecting protection, but the place he regarded as a refuge would become the scene of God’s judgment upon him. The Lord’s deliverance through Jael fulfills Deborah’s earlier warning that Sisera would be handed over to a woman.
