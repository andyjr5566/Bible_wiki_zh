Zechariah sees a lampstand resembling the menorah God commanded for the tabernacle and later stood in the temple (Exodus 25:31–40). Its gold construction reflects holiness, purity, and the glory of God’s presence among His covenant people. The lampstand also points forward to Christ, the true Light of the world, and to the calling of God’s people to bear His light before the nations (John 8:12; Matthew 5:14–16).

Olive oil supplied the lamps of the sanctuary and was used to anoint priests and kings for holy service. In Zechariah’s setting, the two trees correspond to Joshua the high priest and Zerubbabel the governor, God’s appointed servants in the restoration of Jerusalem and the temple after the exile.
