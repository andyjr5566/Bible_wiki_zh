The command recalls the LORD’s words to Moses at the burning bush. Joshua, like Moses, stood before the holy God at a decisive moment in Israel’s history. Removing his sandals expressed reverence, humility, and submission before the One who commissioned and directed him.

Exo 3:5 “Do not come any closer,” God said. “Take off your sandals”…

Act 7:33 Then the Lord said to him, ‘Take off your sandals…

for the place where you are standing is holy.”
The ground became holy because the LORD was present. Near Jericho, at the entrance to the Promised Land, God consecrated Joshua for the task ahead and assured Israel that their inheritance would be secured by His power. The holiness of God required Joshua to approach Him not as an equal or military ally, but as a servant under His command.

Exo 19:12-13 And you are to set up a boundary for the people…

Gen 28:16-17 When Jacob woke up, he said, “Surely the LORD…

And Joshua did so.
Joshua obeyed without hesitation. His submission prepared him to lead Israel according to God’s instructions, including the unexpected strategy for Jericho. Victory in Canaan would come through faith-filled obedience to the LORD, who remained the true Commander of His people.

Exo 40:16 Moses did everything just as the LORD had commanded him.

Gen 6:22 So Noah did everything precisely as God had commanded him.
