Jesus’ delivery to Gentile authority fulfilled His own prediction that He would be handed over to the Gentiles (Mark 10:33).

Mark 15:3

The chief priests, largely drawn from the powerful Sadducean leadership, directed the case against Jesus before Pilate. Having condemned Him on the charge of blasphemy, they now emphasized accusations likely to concern Rome: that Jesus misled the nation, opposed tribute to Caesar, and claimed to be a king (Luke 23:2). Their many charges reveal their determination to secure a Roman execution, since they lacked authority to carry out the death sentence themselves. Jesus stood before false and hostile witnesses without mounting a self-defensive case, fulfilling the portrait of the suffering Servant who was oppressed yet did not open His mouth (Isaiah 53:7). The leaders’ efforts to preserve their own authority became the means by which God’s purpose of redemption moved toward the cross.
