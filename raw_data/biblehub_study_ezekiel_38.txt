A Prophecy against Gog

Ezekiel 38:1

And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,
Ezekiel received this revelation as God’s appointed prophet and messenger. The prophecy follows God’s promises to restore Israel, reunite the nation, and establish His covenant peace in chapters 36–37. God now reveals that hostile powers will yet gather against His restored people under Gog of the land of Magog. The coming conflict will display the LORD’s holiness before the nations, for Israel’s safety will rest not in military strength but in God’s sovereign defense. Ezekiel 38–39 anticipates God’s decisive judgment upon those who oppose His covenant purposes, echoing the final triumph of God over the forces of evil portrayed elsewhere in Scripture.
