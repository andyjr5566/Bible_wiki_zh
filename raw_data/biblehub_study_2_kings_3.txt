Jehoshaphat’s reign in Judah was marked by reforms that called the nation back to the law of Yahweh and by a season of security under God’s blessing. The chronology places Jehoram’s accession near 853–852 BC and prepares for Judah’s later involvement in Israel’s war against Moab.
