And in the seventh month
Hananiah died only two months after his public confrontation with Jeremiah in the fifth month. The seventh month, Tishri, contained Israel’s solemn assemblies and feasts, including the Day of Atonement and the Feast of Tabernacles. During a season that called the nation to repentance and remembrance of God’s faithfulness, Hananiah’s death exposed the deadly seriousness of speaking rebellion in the Lord’s name.

the prophet Hananiah
Hananiah bore the title of prophet and spoke publicly in the temple, yet his message contradicted God’s revealed purpose for Judah. By promising peace without repentance and breaking the symbolic yoke from Jeremiah’s neck, he encouraged resistance to the discipline God had appointed through Babylon. His fate confirmed that religious position and persuasive speech cannot make a message true.
