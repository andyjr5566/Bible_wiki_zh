Job Humbles Himself before the LORD

Job 40:1

And the LORD said to Job:
Yahweh continues His direct address from the whirlwind, bringing Job’s arguments before the Judge of all the earth. After revealing His wisdom and power in creation, God confronts Job’s challenge to divine justice. Job is not given a detailed explanation for his suffering; he is called to humble himself before the God whose rule extends over every part of creation. The exchange prepares Job’s confession of limited understanding and renewed trust in the Lord.
