The Captivity of Ephraim

Isaiah 28:1

Woe to the majestic crown of Ephraim’s drunkards,
Isaiah announces God’s coming judgment against the northern kingdom of Israel, represented by Ephraim. Samaria’s rulers and people gloried in their wealth, security, and position, yet their pride was joined to literal drunkenness and spiritual blindness. Wine had mastered those called to exercise wisdom and restraint, exposing a deeper refusal to submit to the Lord.
