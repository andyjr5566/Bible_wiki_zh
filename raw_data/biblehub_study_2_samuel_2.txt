Asahel, David’s nephew and Joab’s brother, had fallen in the conflict between David’s house and Saul’s house. Abner had warned Asahel to stop pursuing him, yet Asahel refused and was struck down. His body was not abandoned on the battlefield; his brothers and fellow soldiers bore him away with the honor due a faithful warrior and member of David’s family.

Asahel was laid among his fathers in Bethlehem, the town of David’s family and the place from which David had been called to rule.
