The Burden against Nineveh

Nahum 1:1

This is the burden against Nineveh,
Nahum announces God’s weighty message of judgment against the capital of Assyria. Nineveh had once responded to Jonah’s warning with repentance, but later generations returned to violence, idolatry, cruelty, and oppression. Assyria had devastated the northern kingdom of Israel and repeatedly threatened Judah. The city’s apparent strength could not shield it from the righteous Judge, who governs every nation and remembers the bloodshed of the innocent.

the book of the vision of Nahum
God gave Nahum a divinely revealed message, described as a vision, and preserved it in written form for Judah and for later generations. Unlike some prophets who delivered their messages orally, Nahum’s prophecy was recorded in a book, suggesting its importance and permanence. The vision format underscores the supernatural origin of the message, as visions were a common means through which God communicated with His prophets. The prophecy assured God’s people that Assyria’s power was neither ultimate nor permanent. Nineveh fell in 612 BC, demonstrating that the Lord fulfills His warnings and brings down proud kingdoms in His appointed time.
