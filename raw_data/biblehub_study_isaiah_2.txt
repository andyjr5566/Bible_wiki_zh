Isaiah 2:8

Judah’s covenant unfaithfulness had spread throughout the land, even while the temple in Jerusalem remained the center of public worship. The people adopted the gods and practices of surrounding nations, violating God’s command to worship Him alone (Exodus 20:3–4; Judges 2:11–13). Household figurines, shrines, and altars found in the region accord with Isaiah’s portrayal of widespread private idolatry. Such worship was spiritual adultery against the Lord who had redeemed and preserved His people.

Their fall portrays the Lord’s judgment upon every form of human magnificence that exalts itself against Him.
