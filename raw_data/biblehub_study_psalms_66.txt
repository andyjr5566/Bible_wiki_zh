Psalm 66:1

For the choirmaster.
Israel’s worship was ordered for the gathered people of God, with appointed leaders directing singing and instruments in the temple. The psalm’s placement under the choirmaster shows that its praise was intended to be proclaimed publicly, joining individual faith to the worship of the covenant community.

Psalm 66:10

For You, O God, have tested us;
The psalmist speaks for God’s people, recognizing that their afflictions remained under the Lord’s sovereign hand. God tested Israel in the wilderness to humble them, expose their hearts, and teach them dependence upon His word and provision (Deuteronomy 8:2–3). Abraham’s testing on Mount Moriah likewise revealed obedient faith (Genesis 22:1–19). God does not test because He lacks knowledge, but because trials bring faith into visible exercise and call His people to deeper trust.

Israel’s suffering had not been meaningless punishment but a purifying work intended to produce a people devoted to God. The prophets later used the same image for the Lord’s cleansing of His covenant people (Zechariah 13:9; Malachi 3:3). Believers also endure trials that prove and strengthen genuine faith, which is more precious than refined gold (1 Peter 1:6–7). God’s refining work finds its fullest purpose in Christ, whose suffering secured the cleansing and sanctification of His people.

The psalmist testifies that God heard his cry. In the preceding verses, he acknowledges that cherished sin hinders fellowship with God, yet he rejoices that the Lord listened to his prayer. God’s hearing reflects His covenant faithfulness and invites His people to approach Him with repentance, faith, and confidence.

Psa 28:6 Blessed be the LORD, for He has heard my cry for mercy.

Psa 65:2 O You who listen to prayer, all people will come to You.

Psa 34:15 The eyes of the LORD are on the righteous…

or withheld from me His loving devotion!
God’s steadfast love remained with the psalmist through trouble and deliverance. His mercy is not earned by human merit but flows from His faithful character. The Lord’s unfailing love finds its fullest revelation in Jesus Christ, through whom believers are received by God and may draw near to Him in prayer.

Psa 40:11 O LORD, do not withhold Your mercy from me;…

Psa 89:33 But I will not withdraw My loving devotion from him…

Psa 84:11 For the LORD God is a sun and a shield;…
