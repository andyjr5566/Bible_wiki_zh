The river begins beneath the temple threshold (Ezekiel 47:1) and deepens without receiving tributaries, demonstrating a supernatural source. Its course toward the Arabah and the Dead Sea anticipates God’s future restoration of the land and accords with the river of life flowing from God’s throne in Revelation 22:1–2.
