They guarded the way to the tree of life after the fall (Genesis 3:24), overshadowed the mercy seat above the ark (Ex. 25:18–22), and appeared beneath God’s throne-chariot in Ezekiel’s vision (Ezek. 1:4–28; 10:1–20). David portrays the Lord as coming swiftly from heaven to rescue His anointed servant. God is not distant from the dangers of His people; He rules over every power and intervenes with sovereign authority.

Darkness therefore reveals both God’s nearness and His majesty beyond human mastery.
