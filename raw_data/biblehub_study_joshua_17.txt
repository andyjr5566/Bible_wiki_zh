Joshua 17:17

So Joshua said to the house of Joseph—to Ephraim and Manasseh—
Joshua spoke to the two tribes descended from Joseph, whom Jacob had adopted as his own sons and granted a double inheritance among Israel (Genesis 48:5, 22). Ephraim and Manasseh held a prominent place in the nation, and Joshua himself belonged to Ephraim. Their request for additional territory reflected both their growing numbers and the responsibility to establish their families securely in the land God had given them.

Joshua assured the tribes of Ephraim and Manasseh that their inheritance was not limited to the territory already assigned. The mountainous central region of Canaan belonged to Joseph’s descendants by God’s appointment. Its elevation offered protection and strategic strength, while its valleys and slopes could sustain a growing population.
