Israel removed leaven from its homes in obedience to God’s command, remembering both deliverance from bondage and the call to live as a people set apart for Him. The approaching feast would bring great crowds of pilgrims to Jerusalem.

Judas Agrees to Betray Jesus

Satan took a decisive role in moving Judas toward betrayal, though Judas remained morally responsible for yielding to evil. Earlier, the devil had already placed the betrayal in Judas’s heart (John 13:2); now Judas willingly aligned himself with that temptation. The betrayal fulfilled Scripture, including Psalm 41:9, where the trusted companion who shared bread turns against the righteous sufferer. Jesus was not overcome by unforeseen events, but knowingly proceeded toward the cross according to the Father’s redemptive purpose.
