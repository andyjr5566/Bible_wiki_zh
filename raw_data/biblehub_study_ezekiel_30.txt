A Lament for Egypt

Ezekiel 30:1

Again the word of the LORD came to me, saying,
The Lord continued to reveal His purposes through Ezekiel during Judah’s exile in Babylon. Ezekiel did not speak from personal insight or political speculation; he received God’s authoritative message concerning the nations and the unfolding judgment of the day of the LORD. The repeated divine communication testified to God’s faithfulness in warning, judging, and preserving His people. God’s word also prepared Ezekiel to proclaim judgment against Egypt and its allies, showing that no earthly power stands beyond the rule of the Lord. The prophets anticipated the fuller revelation of God in Jesus Christ, the eternal Word made flesh, through whom God has spoken decisively to His people.
