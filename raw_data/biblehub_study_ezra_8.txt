The Exiles Who Returned with Ezra

Ezra 8:1

These are the family heads and genealogical records
Family leadership and verified ancestry established each returnee’s place within Israel’s covenant community. Genealogical records protected inheritance, priestly service, tribal identity, and the continuity of the promises God had given to Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and David. The careful preservation of lineage also prepared the way for the later identification of Jesus Christ as the promised Son of David and rightful Messiah.
