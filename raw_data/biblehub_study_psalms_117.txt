Extol Him, All You Peoples

Psalm 117:1

Praise the LORD, all you nations!
Israel’s God summons every nation to worship Him. Though the LORD uniquely revealed Himself through Israel and established His covenant with Abraham, His purpose always included the blessing of all families of the earth through Abraham’s promised Seed, Jesus Christ (Genesis 12:3; Galatians 3:16). Israel’s place among surrounding empires testified that the God of Israel alone rules every people and king. Paul cites this call in Romans 15:11 as evidence that Gentile inclusion in the salvation of Christ fulfills Scripture. The command anticipates the Great Commission and the future multitude from every nation, tribe, people, and language standing before God’s throne (Matthew 28:19; Revelation 7:9).

Psa 86:9 All the nations You have made will come and bow before You…

Extol Him, all you peoples!
Every ethnic group is invited to render joyful, public honor to the LORD. Worship belongs to Him not because all cultures are identical, but because all humanity shares one Creator, stands under His righteous rule, and may receive mercy through His Son. The repeated summons strengthens the universal scope of God’s redemptive purpose: the nations are not merely observers of Israel’s God but are called to join His redeemed people in praise. The day will come when every knee bows and every tongue confesses that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father (Philippians 2:10–11).

Psalm 117:2

For great is His loving devotion toward us,
God’s covenant love sustained Israel through deliverance from Egypt, preservation in the wilderness, discipline for sin, and restoration after exile. His mercy did not rest on Israel’s merit but on His own covenant purpose. In Jesus Christ, God’s steadfast love is revealed fully, as He gave His Son for sinners and gathers a people from every nation into His covenant family.

Psa 86:13 For great is Your loving devotion to me;…

Psa 103:11 For as high as the heavens are above the earth,…

Psa 36:5 Your loving devotion, O LORD, reaches to the heavens,…

and the faithfulness of the LORD endures forever.
The Lord fulfills every promise He makes and remains true despite human unfaithfulness. His faithfulness guarded the promises given to Abraham, preserved David’s royal line, and brought the promised Messiah into the world. Christ’s resurrection confirms that God’s saving purpose cannot fail, giving His people lasting confidence in His promises.

Hallelujah!
Praise, expressed in “Hallelujah,” meaning “Praise the LORD,” is the fitting response to the Lord’s unchanging mercy and reliability. The psalm calls all peoples to join Israel in worship, anticipating the redeemed multitude from every nation who will praise God forever.
