The Lesson of the Lamp

Luke 8:16

No one lights a lamp and covers it with a jar
A lamp was a small clay vessel fueled by oil, designed to give light within a dark house. Covering it would extinguish or conceal the very light for which it had been lit. Jesus had just explained the parable of the sower to His disciples, revealing truths about the kingdom that many others did not yet understand. Divine revelation is not given to be buried but to make known the saving truth of God.

Mat 5:15 Neither do people light a lamp and put it under a basket.…

Luk 11:33 No one lights a lamp and puts it in a cellar…

Jhn 5:35 John was a lamp that burned and gave light,…

or puts it under a bed.
