1 Chronicles 29:12

Both riches and honor come from You,
David confessed before the assembled leaders of Israel that every possession, achievement, and position of dignity ultimately comes from God. As Israel prepared for Solomon’s reign and the building of the temple, David refused to credit human skill, military success, or royal authority for the nation’s prosperity. Deuteronomy 8:18 similarly warns that the ability to gain wealth is God’s gift. Riches and honor therefore carry a stewardship responsibility; they are received from the Lord and must be used for His purposes. Wisdom, which begins with the fear of the Lord, is also associated with enduring honor rather than temporary worldly success (Proverbs 3:16).

Israel’s rise among these kingdoms displayed God’s faithfulness to His promises and anticipated the universal dominion of the coming Messiah, whose kingdom will extend over all nations.
