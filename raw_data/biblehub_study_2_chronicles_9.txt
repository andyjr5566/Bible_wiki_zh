This amount represented more than fifty thousand pounds of gold, apart from revenue from merchants, traders, and foreign kings (2 Chronicles 9:14). The record emphasizes the extraordinary prosperity of Solomon’s kingdom, yet it also anticipates its spiritual danger. Rehoboam inherited the throne according to the Lord’s promise that David’s descendants would continue to rule in Jerusalem. Yet his rejection of wise counsel and harsh response to Israel’s request for relief led to the division of the kingdom. Judah remained under David’s house, while the northern tribes revolted under Jeroboam, fulfilling the judgment God had announced because of Solomon’s unfaithfulness.
