Amaziah Reigns in Judah

2 Chronicles 25:1

Amaziah was twenty-five years old when he became king,
Amaziah succeeded his father Joash after Joash was murdered by his own servants (2 Chronicles 24:25). At twenty-five, Amaziah was old enough to assume the responsibilities of David’s throne and to begin establishing his own rule. His accession came at a critical moment for Judah, which had declined spiritually after the death of Jehoiada the priest. The age also recalls the beginning of Levitical service at twenty-five (Numbers 8:24), an age associated with mature responsibility before the Lord.

and he reigned in Jerusalem twenty-nine years.
Jerusalem remained the center of Judah’s government, worship, and covenant identity because the temple stood there and the Lord had chosen the city for His name. Amaziah’s reign included the punishment of his father’s assassins, victory over Edom, a disastrous war against Israel, and later conspiracy against himself. His years on the throne reveal that outward success cannot preserve a king who turns from wholehearted dependence on God.

2 Chronicles 25:2

And he did what was right in the eyes of the LORD,
Amaziah began his reign with acts of obedience, including judging his father’s assassins while sparing their children according to the Law of Moses (2 Chronicles 25:3–4). He also heeded God’s warning through a prophet and dismissed the soldiers he had hired from Israel (2 Chronicles 25:7–10). His conduct showed real recognition of God’s authority and of the covenant obligations of Judah’s king.
