1 Samuel 4:19

Now Eli’s daughter-in-law, the wife of Phinehas, was pregnant and about to give birth.
Phinehas’s wife belonged to Eli’s priestly household at Shiloh, a family entrusted with service before the Lord but brought under judgment because of Eli’s sons’ persistent corruption. Her unnamed condition underscores the devastation that would soon fall upon the whole house of Eli. The imminent birth of a child ordinarily promised continuity and hope, yet this child would be born as the priestly family and the nation endured calamity.

The Philistines’ capture of the ark represented far more than a military defeat.
