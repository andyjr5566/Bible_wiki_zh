Those who had become ceremonially unclean sought purification before taking part in the feast and its sacrifices. The Law required cleansing from defilement, including contact with a dead body, before entering the holy precincts and participating in worship (Numbers 19:11–20). As worshipers prepared outwardly for Passover, the One who alone could cleanse the conscience from sin was drawing near to offer Himself for them.
