The angelic messenger recalls his activity during the first year of Darius the Mede, immediately after Babylon fell to the Medo-Persian forces in 539 BC. Darius the Mede remains debated in historical identification, though the biblical record presents him as the ruler placed over Babylon before Cyrus exercised full authority. The change of empire marked a decisive turning point for Judah, since the Persian administration soon permitted Jewish exiles to return to Jerusalem and rebuild the temple, fulfilling God’s promises through Isaiah and Jeremiah.
