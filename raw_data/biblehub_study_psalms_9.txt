His refuge is not merely escape from danger but the security of His righteous rule. The name of God is a strong tower (Proverbs 18:10), providing safety and security to those who understand and call upon it.

Psalm 9:12

For the Avenger of bloodshed remembers;
God does not overlook innocent blood or leave murder and oppression unanswered. Israel’s law assigned the avenger of blood, usually a near relative, to seek justice for a slain family member within carefully defined legal boundaries (Numbers 35:19–28). God stands above every human court as the righteous Judge who knows hidden violence, false testimony, and unpunished crimes. Abel’s blood cried out from the ground, and God acted against Cain (Genesis 4:10–11). Divine vengeance is never reckless retaliation; it is the holy judgment of the One who values every life made in His image.
