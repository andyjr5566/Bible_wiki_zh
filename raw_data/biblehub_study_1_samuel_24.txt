1 Samuel 24:21

So now, swear to me by the LORD
Saul calls David to make a binding oath before the God of Israel, appealing to the Lord as witness and judge. Oaths made in the Lord’s name carried sacred responsibility, for Israel was commanded to fulfill every vow made before God (Numbers 30:2). Saul’s request acknowledges David’s integrity and recognizes that David’s future is governed by the Lord rather than by human ambition.
