Joshua 9:5

The Gibeonites deliberately equipped themselves to appear as travelers from a distant country rather than inhabitants of Canaan. Their worn footwear supported a carefully constructed lie designed to secure a covenant with Israel and escape the judgment falling on the land. Israel had been commanded not to make treaties with the peoples of Canaan (Deuteronomy 7:2), yet the visible evidence persuaded the leaders because they did not seek the Lord’s counsel (Joshua 9:14). Outward signs can be convincing while concealing a false heart and purpose; the Lord sees what human eyes cannot discern (1 Samuel 16:7).

Their scheme resembles other biblical deceptions in which external appearances were manipulated to secure an advantage, including Jacob’s deception of Isaac (Genesis 27).
