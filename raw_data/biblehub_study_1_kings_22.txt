Edom’s lack of an independent king helped secure Judah’s southern approaches and gave Jehoshaphat access to the Gulf of Aqaba.

1 Kings 22:48

Jehoshaphat built ships of Tarshish
Jehoshaphat sought to develop Judah’s commerce through a fleet of large, ocean-going vessels. Ezion-geber, on the Gulf of Aqaba, had served as Solomon’s southern port and provided access to Red Sea trade routes. Judah’s prosperity under Jehoshaphat reflected God’s blessing, yet commercial strength could not secure success apart from faithful dependence on the Lord.

1 Kings 22:49

Ahaziah ruled Israel after Ahab and continued the idolatrous course established by his father and Jezebel. Jehoshaphat had previously entered an unwise alliance with Ahab, including the campaign at Ramoth-gilead, yet he had returned to Judah under God’s mercy and renewed his efforts to lead the nation in righteousness. Ahaziah’s approach came after Jehoshaphat’s trading fleet had been wrecked at Ezion-geber, near the Gulf of Aqaba. The failed venture had already been identified by the prophet Eliezer as God’s judgment on Jehoshaphat’s partnership with Ahaziah (2 Chronicles 20:35–37).

1 Kings 22:50

And Jehoshaphat rested with his fathers
Jehoshaphat’s death ended a reign marked by devotion to the Lord, judicial reforms, and efforts to strengthen Judah in the knowledge of God’s law (2 Chronicles 17:7–9; 19:4–11). Though he made the harmful alliance with Ahab’s house, the Lord preserved Judah and granted him many years of relative stability. His death also reminds Israel that even godly kings pass away, while the covenant faithfulness of the Lord endures.

1 Kings 22:53

Ahaziah served and worshiped Baal,
Ahaziah followed the idolatrous course established by Ahab and Jezebel. Baal worship, imported and promoted through Jezebel’s Phoenician influence, honored a false god associated with fertility, rain, and storms. Israel’s dependence on Baal for agricultural prosperity directly contradicted its covenant dependence on the LORD, who alone gives rain, harvest, and life. By serving Baal, Ahaziah violated the first commandment and continued the apostasy Elijah had confronted at Mount Carmel.
