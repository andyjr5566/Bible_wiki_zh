David Anointed King of All Israel

2 Samuel 5:1

Then all the tribes of Israel came to David at Hebron
Hebron, where David had ruled Judah for seven and a half years, became the place where the division following Saul’s death was brought to an end. The city carried deep covenant significance as the place where Abraham dwelt near the oaks of Mamre and built an altar to the Lord (Genesis 13:18). Israel’s elders came in united recognition that the Lord had chosen David, fulfilling Samuel’s earlier anointing of Jesse’s son (1 Samuel 16:1–13). David’s reign over the whole nation anticipated the greater reign of Christ, the Son of David, who will gather His people under one King.
