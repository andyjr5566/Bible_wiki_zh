Assyria, Babylon, and other dominant powers had exalted themselves against the Lord and oppressed His people, yet no human strength can finally withstand Him. God’s acts in history anticipate the day when all peoples will confess the lordship of Jesus Christ.

Zion’s security does not arise from military strength but from the presence of the Great King. Isaiah’s vision reaches beyond Jerusalem’s immediate history to the glorious kingdom in which the nations gather to the Root of Jesse and God dwells with His people.
