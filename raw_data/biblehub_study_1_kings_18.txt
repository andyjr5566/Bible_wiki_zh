Jezreel would later become associated with Ahab’s injustice against Naboth and with God’s judgment upon his dynasty (1 Kings 21:1–24; 2 Kings 9:30–37). The king departed under God’s mercy, while Elijah remained the faithful servant of the Lord in a nation whose leaders continued to resist Him.
