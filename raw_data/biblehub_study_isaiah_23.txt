The Burden against Tyre

Isaiah 23:1

This is the burden against Tyre:
Isaiah announces God’s judgment upon Tyre, the wealthy Phoenician commercial center on the Mediterranean coast. Its influence rested on maritime trade, fortified security, and accumulated riches, yet none of these could shield it from the Lord’s rule over nations. Tyre’s downfall exposed the emptiness of pride built on prosperity and human achievement.

and sell herself to all the kingdoms on the face of the earth.
Tyre’s ships and merchants reached widely across the Mediterranean world, distributing luxury goods, purple cloth, metals, timber, and other merchandise. Her return to global trade fulfilled the prophecy while exposing the emptiness of prosperity pursued without reverence for God. Yet Isaiah’s later promise shows that the Lord could ultimately direct even Tyre’s wealth for the benefit of those who dwell before Him.

Isaiah 23:18

Yet her profits and wages will be set apart to the LORD;
Tyre’s commercial wealth, once gained for pride and self-exaltation, will ultimately come under the Lord’s authority. The God who judges nations also claims their resources for His purposes. Tyre’s history of maritime trade and its connections with Israel, including Hiram’s assistance in Solomon’s building projects, anticipates a day when the nations’ wealth will serve the worship of the true God.

Those who dwell before the Lord are His people, especially those gathered in and around Jerusalem to worship and serve Him. The picture reaches beyond Tyre’s restoration to the wider promise that the nations will bring their glory to Zion and acknowledge the Lord’s reign.
