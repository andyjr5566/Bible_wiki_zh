Ben-hadad Attacks Samaria

1 Kings 20:1

Now Ben-hadad king of Aram assembled his entire army.
Ben-hadad, likely Ben-hadad II, ruled Aram from Damascus, Israel’s powerful northeastern neighbor. His name, meaning “son of Hadad,” reflected the worship of Hadad, the Aramean storm god. By gathering his full military strength, Ben-hadad sought not merely a border victory but Israel’s submission. The crisis exposed Ahab’s kingdom to a force beyond its natural ability to resist and prepared the way for the Lord to reveal His authority over Israel’s enemies.
