War between Asa and Baasha

1 Kings 15:16

Now there was war between Asa and Baasha king of Israel
Asa ruled Judah from about 911 to 870 BC, while Baasha ruled Israel from about 909 to 886 BC. Their conflict grew out of the division of Solomon’s kingdom, a division rooted in covenant unfaithfulness and preserved by rival political and religious loyalties. Asa had led Judah in reforms that opposed idolatry, whereas Baasha continued the sinful course established by Jeroboam. The hostility soon centered on control of border regions and access between the two kingdoms.

The enduring conflict revealed the bitter consequences of Israel’s divided worship and divided allegiance. Neither military strength nor political alliances could provide the peace that comes from wholehearted faithfulness to the Lord.

His reign demonstrated that removing a wicked dynasty does not itself bring spiritual renewal. The LORD saw the public policies, private motives, and covenant rebellion that marked Baasha’s rule.

1Ki 14:22 And Judah did evil in the sight of the LORD,…

2Ki 21:2 And he did evil in the sight of the LORD…

Jdg 2:11 And the Israelites did evil in the sight of the LORD…

and walked in the way of Jeroboam
Jeroboam had established rival worship centers at Bethel and Dan, using golden calves to keep the northern tribes from worshiping at Jerusalem. Baasha preserved that system rather than leading Israel according to the law of God. Political security became more important than faithful worship, and Jeroboam’s rebellion continued through a new dynasty.

1Ki 16:26 For he walked in all the ways of Jeroboam son of Nebat…

1Ki 22:52 And he did evil in the sight of the LORD…

2Ki 13:2 And he did evil in the sight of the LORD…

and in his sin, which he had caused Israel to commit.
Baasha bore personal guilt for maintaining idolatry and corporate responsibility for directing the nation in it. Israel’s kings were accountable not only for their own conduct but also for the spiritual path they set before the people. Baasha’s persistence in Jeroboam’s sin brought God’s judgment upon his own house, as later announced through the prophet Jehu.

1Ki 14:16 So He will give Israel over on account of the sins…

2Ki 17:21 When the LORD had torn Israel away from the house of David,…

2Ki 17:22 The Israelites persisted in all the sins…
