Jeremiah speaks under the direct authority of the covenant God of Israel. The warning comes from the LORD Himself, not from the prophet’s own judgment or political opinion. God’s word carries special weight because He is the righteous King over Judah and its earthly rulers. The prophetic books repeatedly present such announcements as divine summonses to hear, repent, and obey.
