Nebuchadnezzar Confesses God’s Kingdom

Daniel 4:1

King Nebuchadnezzar,
Nebuchadnezzar II reigned over Babylon from 605 to 562 BC and built Babylon into the dominant empire of the ancient Near East. After conquering Judah and carrying many Jews into exile, he encountered the God of Israel through Daniel and the events recorded in Daniel 1–4. The royal proclamation that follows reveals a ruler humbled by God after displaying extraordinary pride and power.

To the people of every nation and language
Babylon governed a vast and ethnically diverse empire, enlarged through conquest and the forced relocation of conquered peoples. Nebuchadnezzar addresses his subjects in the broad language of imperial authority, yet his testimony declares that the God of heaven rules over every people and ruler. God’s kingdom, unlike Babylon’s, has no geographical or historical boundary.
