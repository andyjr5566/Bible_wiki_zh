Barzillai, whose name means “iron-hearted,” reflecting his strong character, was a wealthy, respected leader from Gilead east of the Jordan. During David’s flight from Absalom, he had supplied the king and his followers with food and provisions when they were exhausted and vulnerable (2 Samuel 17:27–29). His support required courage, since Absalom then controlled Jerusalem and David’s future appeared uncertain. Barzillai’s loyalty rested not on political advantage but on faithfulness to the Lord’s anointed king.
