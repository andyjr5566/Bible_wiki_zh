On the first day of the seventh month,
The first day of Tishri was appointed as the Feast of Trumpets, a sacred day marked by trumpet blasts, rest, and holy assembly (Leviticus 23:23–25; Numbers 29:1). Later Jewish tradition identifies this season with Rosh Hashanah, the beginning of the civil year. The month also contained the Day of Atonement and the Feast of Tabernacles, making it a fitting time for the restored community to hear God’s covenant instruction and renew its obedience.
