Jericho stood as the first fortified obstacle in the land, yet Israel entered Canaan by God’s power before any battle was fought. The crossing prepared the nation for the conquest and taught them that victory would come through obedience and trust in the Lord. Passing through the waters into a new life in the promised land also foreshadows the believer’s identification with God’s saving work and a life set apart for His purposes.

Joshua 3:17

The priests carrying the ark of the covenant of the LORD
The Ark represented the holy presence of the covenant-making God among His people. It was associated with the tablets of the covenant, Aaron’s rod, and the pot of manna, bearing witness to God’s law, provision, and appointed priesthood. The priests carried it according to God’s command, showing that Israel’s advance into Canaan depended upon the LORD’s presence rather than military strength.

The Jordan had overflowed its banks at harvest time, yet the waters ceased when the priests entered it. Psa 114:3 The sea observed and fled; the Jordan turned back;

while all Israel crossed over the dry ground,
Every Israelite crossed safely, from the strongest to the most vulnerable. The dry riverbed left no doubt that the passage came by divine intervention. Israel entered the land as one covenant people, led by the presence of God and sustained by His faithfulness. The event points forward to the greater deliverance accomplished by Christ, who brings His people from bondage into the promised inheritance of eternal life.
