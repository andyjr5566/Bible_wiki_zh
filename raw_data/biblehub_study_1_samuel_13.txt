1 Samuel 13:1

The Hebrew text at this point is difficult, and the exact age of Saul at his accession cannot be established with certainty. Saul was nevertheless a mature man when Samuel anointed him, already recognized for his physical stature and capable of leading Israel in battle. His appointment marked Israel’s transition from the rule of judges to a human monarchy, granted in response to the people’s demand for a king like the surrounding nations.

God preserved His covenant purposes by preparing David to succeed Saul, showing that lasting leadership in Israel depended not on outward strength or public approval but on faithful submission to the Lord.
