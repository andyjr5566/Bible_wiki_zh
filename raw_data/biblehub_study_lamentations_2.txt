God’s Anger over Jerusalem

Lamentations 2:1

How the Lord has covered the Daughter of Zion with the cloud of His anger!
Jerusalem, called the Daughter of Zion, lay under God’s judgment because Judah had persistently broken His covenant through idolatry, injustice, and refusal to heed His prophets. The cloud portrays not the protecting presence that led Israel through the wilderness (Exodus 13:21), but the darkness of divine wrath that concealed hope and brought calamity. Babylon’s siege and destruction of Jerusalem in 586 BC fulfilled warnings given through Jeremiah and other prophets.

The Temple’s destruction proved that religious buildings and rituals cannot preserve a people who reject God’s commands.
