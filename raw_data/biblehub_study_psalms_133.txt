How Pleasant to Live in Harmony!

Psalm 133:1

A song of ascents.
Psalm 133 belongs to Psalms 120–134, sung by Israelites traveling to Jerusalem for Passover, Pentecost, and Tabernacles. As worshipers ascended to the city where the temple stood, they confessed their dependence on the Lord and anticipated fellowship with His people. Jerusalem’s elevation made the journey upward in a literal sense, while the pilgrimage also directed hearts toward God’s dwelling place.

Psa 120:1 A song of ascents. In my distress I cried to the LORD,…

Psa 121:1 A song of ascents. I lift up my eyes to the hills.…

Psa 122:1 I was glad when they said to me, “Let us go…

Of David.
David knew both the division and the gathering of Israel. After becoming king, he united the tribes, established Jerusalem as the national capital, and brought the ark of God there. His song celebrates the blessing of a people joined in faithful worship under the Lord’s appointed rule.

Psa 3:1 A Psalm of David, when he fled from his son Absalom.…

Psa 18:1 For the choirmaster. Of David the servant of the LORD…

Psa 23:1 A Psalm of David. The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want.

Behold,
God’s people are summoned to give careful attention to a blessing that is easily neglected amid rivalry, pride, and division.

Gen 1:31 And God looked upon all that He had made…

Luk 2:10 But the angel said to them, “Do not be afraid!…

Rev 21:5 And the One seated on the throne said, “Behold,…

how good and pleasant it is
Unity among those who belong to God is morally right and deeply joyful. It reflects the goodness of the Lord, who created His people not for isolation or strife but for shared life, worship, and obedience.

Psa 147:1 Hallelujah! How good it is to sing praises to our God…

Pro 3:17 All her ways are pleasant, and all her paths are peaceful.

Ecc 5:18 Here is what I have seen to be good and fitting:…

when brothers live together in harmony!
Israel’s tribes, descended from Jacob’s sons, were bound together by covenant and by the worship of the one true God. Their unity required more than living near one another; it called for peace, mutual faithfulness, and submission to the Lord. In Christ, believers are brought into one family and commanded to preserve the unity produced by the Spirit. Such harmony anticipates the final gathering of God’s redeemed people under the reign of the Prince of Peace.

Rom 12:16 Live in harmony with one another. Do not be proud.…

Act 4:32 The multitude of believers was one in heart and soul.…

Eph 4:3 and with diligence to preserve the unity…

Psalm 133:2

It is like fine oil on the head,
Precious oil was used in Israel’s sacred anointing rites to set apart those appointed for God’s service. The holy anointing oil prepared for the tabernacle was unique and not to be used casually, marking its recipient as consecrated to the Lord (Exodus 30:22–33). Its richness portrays God’s favor as holy, costly, and life-giving.

Lev 8:12 He also poured some of the anointing oil on Aaron’s head…

Exo 29:7 Then take the anointing oil and anoint him…

Psa 23:5 You prepare a table before me in the presence of my enemies.…

running down on the beard,
The oil did not rest in a small amount upon Aaron’s head but flowed freely, portraying abundant blessing. In a culture where a beard represented honor and maturity, the flowing oil displays the dignity God bestowed on His appointed servant. The unity celebrated in Psalm 133 is therefore not manufactured agreement but a gracious blessing poured out by God.

1Sa 21:13 So he changed his behavior before them and feigned madness…

Lev 21:5 Priests must not make bald spots on their heads,…

running down Aaron’s beard
Aaron was Israel’s first high priest, consecrated by Moses according to God’s command (Leviticus 8:12). As high priest, he represented the people before the Lord and served in the sacrifices and worship of the tabernacle. His anointing anticipates Jesus Christ, the perfect and eternal High Priest, who brings His people into fellowship with God (Hebrews 4:14–16).

Lev 8:30 Next, Moses took some of the anointing oil…

Exo 30:30 Anoint Aaron and his sons and consecrate them…

Lev 21:12 He must not leave or desecrate the sanctuary of his God,…

over the collar of his robes.
Aaron’s priestly garments were made for glory and beauty and bore reminders of his responsibility to represent Israel before God (Exodus 28). Oil flowing from his head onto his garments shows an anointing that covered his whole person and ministry. In the same way, God’s blessing upon His people is meant to extend through the whole community, joining them in holy worship and faithful service.

Exo 28:31-32 You are to make the robe of the ephod entirely of blue cloth…

Exo 39:22-23 They made the robe of the ephod entirely of blue cloth…

Psalm 133:3

It is like the dew of Hermon
Mount Hermon, rising in Israel’s far north, received abundant moisture that sustained its vegetation through dry seasons. Its dew pictured refreshing, life-giving provision from God in a land dependent upon His care.

Deu 33:13 Concerning Joseph he said: “May his land be blessed…

Mic 5:7 Then the remnant of Jacob will be like dew from the LORD,…

Hos 14:5 I will be like the dew to Israel; he will blossom…

falling on the mountains of Zion.
Though Hermon lay far north of Jerusalem, the image portrays God’s abundant blessing reaching Zion, the place of His chosen dwelling and the center of Israel’s worship. As dew revives the earth, godly unity refreshes God’s people and displays His purpose to gather them before Him.

Psa 87:1-3 A Psalm of the sons of Korah. A song. He has founded…

Isa 2:2-3 In the last days the mountain of the house of the LORD…

Heb 12:22 Instead, you have come to Mount Zion, to the city…

For there the LORD has bestowed the blessing
The LORD Himself commands and grants blessing among a people dwelling together in faithful unity. Zion represented the place where Israel came before God, and unity among those who worship Him accords with His holy presence.

Psa 134:3 May the LORD, the Maker of heaven and earth…

Psa 128:5 May the LORD bless you from Zion, that you may see…

Psa 132:13-16 For the LORD has chosen Zion; He has desired it…

of life forevermore.
God’s blessing brings enduring life under His favor, reaching beyond earthly prosperity to eternal fellowship with Him. The fullness of that promise is revealed in Jesus Christ, who gives eternal life to all who belong to Him.

Dan 12:2 And many who sleep in the dust of the earth will awake…

Jhn 17:3 Now this is eternal life, that they may know You…

1Jn 2:25 And this is the promise that He Himself made to us…
