Ezekiel 35:1

Ezekiel received another direct revelation from the LORD during the Babylonian exile. God continued to speak to His covenant people through His prophet despite Jerusalem’s fall and the nation’s displacement. The message introduces the judgment pronounced against Mount Seir, the homeland of Edom, for its longstanding hostility toward Israel and its actions during Judah’s calamity. The coming oracle affirms God’s authority over Israel and the surrounding nations, holding each accountable for rebellion, violence, and pride.
