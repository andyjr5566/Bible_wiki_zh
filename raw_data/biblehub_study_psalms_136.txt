Psalm 136:10

He struck down the firstborn of Egypt
At midnight the LORD brought judgment on every Egyptian household, from Pharaoh’s firstborn to the firstborn of the prisoner and of the livestock (Exodus 12:29–30). Egypt’s gods, including Pharaoh’s claimed divinity, could neither avert nor answer this judgment. Israel was spared only where the blood of the Passover lamb marked the doorposts, showing that deliverance came through God’s appointed sacrifice. The plague broke Pharaoh’s resistance and opened the way for Israel’s Exodus from slavery. Passover points forward to Christ, the Lamb of God, whose blood delivers His people from judgment and brings them into lasting freedom.

He struck down great kings
God demonstrated His absolute authority by defeating powerful rulers who opposed Israel’s progress toward the Promised Land. Sihon king of the Amorites and Og king of Bashan ruled fortified territories east of the Jordan, yet neither military strength nor giant stature could resist the Lord’s purpose (Numbers 21:21–35; Deuteronomy 3:1–11). Their defeat secured land for the tribes of Reuben, Gad, and half of Manasseh, while confirming that Israel’s inheritance came by God’s power rather than human ability. Earthly rulers remain accountable to the King above all kings, who humbles pride and fulfills His covenant promises.

Psalm 136:25

God sustains the life He created, providing nourishment for mankind, livestock, birds, and every living thing. Israel’s harvests depended upon the Lord’s rain, seasons, and blessing, reminding the nation that bread ultimately came from His hand. The same Creator who gave vegetation for food in the beginning continues to uphold the order of creation. His loving devotion endures forever.
God’s provision is not occasional generosity but the continual outworking of His covenant faithfulness. Psalm 136 joins the care of creation with the Lord’s mighty acts in Israel’s history, including the exodus, the wilderness journey, and the gift of the promised land. His people could remember that the God who delivered them also sustained them day by day. That enduring love reaches its fullest display in the giving of His Son, whose death and resurrection secure forgiveness and everlasting life for all who believe.
