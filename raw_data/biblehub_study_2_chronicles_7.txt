The LORD’s Response to Solomon

2 Chronicles 7:11

Solomon completed the temple as the dwelling place chosen for the name of the LORD and the palace as the administrative center of Israel’s kingdom. The temple fulfilled the Lord’s promise to David that his son would build a house for God’s name (2 Samuel 7:12–13). Its completion followed seven years of construction, while the royal complex required thirteen years (1 Kings 6:38; 7:1). Together, these works displayed the peace, resources, and order God had granted to David’s son. Yet the temple’s grandeur did not replace the need for faithful worship and obedience.
