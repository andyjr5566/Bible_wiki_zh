Its famed cedar forests, long prized for royal palaces and for Solomon’s Temple (1 Kings 5:6; 2 Chronicles 2:8), represented strength, splendor, and established power. The opened doors portray defenses removed before the Lord’s appointed judgment.

The judgment moving from Lebanon through Bashan and into the land points toward the ruin that would fall upon Israel’s corrupt shepherds and culminate in the destruction associated with their rejection of the true Shepherd.

The imagery portrays ruthless destruction, leaving the flock wounded and unable to stand or escape. Rejecting God’s Shepherd leaves people exposed to leaders who destroy rather than protect, while Christ preserves His sheep securely in His hand (John 10:28).

Zechariah 11:17

Woe to the worthless shepherd,
God pronounces judgment on the leader who abandons the sacred trust of caring for His people. Zechariah’s shepherd imagery exposes rulers, priests, and prophets who used the flock for themselves rather than leading the nation in faithfulness. The worthless shepherd stands in deliberate contrast to the coming good Shepherd, Jesus Christ, who lays down His life for the sheep.

Ezk 34:2-4 Son of man, prophesy against the shepherds of Israel…

who deserts the flock!
Rather than protecting the vulnerable from danger, this shepherd leaves them exposed. Israel had repeatedly suffered under leaders who pursued power, wealth, and foreign alliances while neglecting justice, truth, and worship of the Lord. Ezekiel 34 similarly condemns shepherds who fed themselves instead of the flock. The pattern reaches its fullest expression in the false ruler who will deceive and oppress many before Christ’s return.

May a sword strike his arm and his right eye!
The sword of divine judgment falls upon the very faculties by which a shepherd would serve. His arm, representing strength and authority, will no longer be able to act. His right eye, representing discernment and direction, will no longer be able to see clearly. The leader who misused power and led others into blindness will be stripped of both power and insight.
