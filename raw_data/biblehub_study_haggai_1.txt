Haggai 1:1

Darius I of Persia reigned from 522 to 486 BC, placing Haggai’s ministry in 520 BC. Nearly two decades had passed since Cyrus permitted Jewish exiles to return to Jerusalem. Although the altar had been restored and the temple foundation laid, rebuilding had languished while the people established their own homes and livelihoods.
