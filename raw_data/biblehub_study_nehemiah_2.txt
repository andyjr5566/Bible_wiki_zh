Jerusalem’s restoration threatened their political influence and economic interests. Their alliance brought together neighboring peoples with long-standing hostility toward a strong and faithful Jewish community in Jerusalem. Similar resistance had hindered the rebuilding efforts in Ezra’s day (Ezra 4:1–5).
