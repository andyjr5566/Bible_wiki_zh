David convened the tribal heads, military commanders, administrators, and other national officials to establish an orderly transition to Solomon’s reign. As Moses and Joshua had assembled Israel for covenant instruction and decisive national moments, David brought the nation’s leadership together to unite them around the work God had appointed: the building of the temple and the worship of the Lord in Jerusalem. Israel’s future depended not merely on political stability but on faithful submission to God’s revealed purposes.

The ordered service of the Levites would support the ministry of the priests and help preserve the nation’s devotion to the Lord. The Gershonites:
The Gershonites descended from Gershon, Levi’s eldest son, and formed one of the three principal Levitical divisions alongside the Kohathites and Merarites. In the wilderness they carried and cared for the tabernacle curtains, coverings, screens, and cords (Numbers 3:25–26). Their assigned service guarded the order and holiness of Israel’s worship, showing that every part of the sanctuary and every act of service belonged to the Lord.
