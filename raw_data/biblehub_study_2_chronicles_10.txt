Rebellion against Rehoboam

2 Chronicles 10:1

Then Rehoboam went to Shechem,
Rehoboam, Solomon’s son and David’s grandson, traveled to Shechem at the beginning of his reign. Shechem stood in the hill country of Ephraim and had long been a significant national gathering place. Abraham built an altar there, Jacob called his household to put away foreign gods there, and Joshua renewed Israel’s covenant with the Lord there (Genesis 12:6–7; Genesis 35:4; Joshua 24:1–25). By appearing at Shechem rather than remaining in Jerusalem, Rehoboam sought recognition from the northern tribes as well as Judah.
