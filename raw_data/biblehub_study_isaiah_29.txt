Woe to David’s City

Isaiah 29:1

Woe to you, O Ariel,
Ariel, meaning lion of God or altar hearth, identifies Jerusalem, a city marked by God’s presence, David’s throne, and the altar of sacrifice. The woe, a pronouncement of judgment or lament, announces approaching judgment upon a people whose privileged place before God had not produced faithful obedience.

Jerusalem’s historic connection to David made its spiritual rebellion especially grievous, since the city was called to reflect God’s rule among His people.
