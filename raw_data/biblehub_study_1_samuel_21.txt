The Lord preserved David during a season when Saul sought his life and the Philistines recognized him as Israel’s champion.

Achish refused to receive David into his royal residence, dismissing him rather than treating him as an enemy worthy of attention. David left Gath alive and soon found refuge in the cave of Adullam. The rejected fugitive would later become Israel’s king, establishing the house of David from which the promised Messiah would come.
