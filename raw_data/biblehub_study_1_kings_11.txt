Solomon’s Foreign Wives

1 Kings 11:1

King Solomon, however, loved many foreign women
Solomon’s wisdom and wealth did not preserve him from disobedience when his affections were divided from wholehearted devotion to the Lord. His marriages served political purposes, strengthening alliances and extending influence, but God had warned Israel not to intermarry with idolatrous nations because such unions would turn their hearts toward other gods (Deuteronomy 7:3–4). Israel’s king was also forbidden to multiply wives lest his heart turn away (Deuteronomy 17:17). Solomon’s failure in this area prepared the way for the spiritual decline and eventual division of the kingdom.
