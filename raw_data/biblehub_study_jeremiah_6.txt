Jerusalem’s Final Warning

Jeremiah 6:1

“Run for cover, O sons of Benjamin;
Benjamin’s territory bordered Jerusalem on the north, placing its people directly in the path of an invading army approaching Judah. The tribe had a history of skilled and courageous warriors, yet military strength could not shield a people under God’s judgment. The warning demanded immediate flight rather than confidence in inherited privilege or human defenses.

What appeared to be the movement of empires was also the outworking of the Lord’s righteous decree.

Yet Jerusalem’s judgment also stands within God’s larger purpose of restoration, ultimately fulfilled through the Messiah, who redeems a people purified by grace and called to faithful worship.
