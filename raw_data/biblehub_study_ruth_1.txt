The Return to Bethlehem

Ruth 1:19

So Naomi and Ruth traveled until they came to Bethlehem.
Naomi and Ruth completed the difficult journey from Moab to Bethlehem, traveling through rugged country and returning to the land God had given Israel. Bethlehem, “House of Bread,” had been Naomi’s home before famine drove her family to Moab. Her return marks a movement from bereavement and exile toward the place of God’s covenant provision. Ruth entered Judah as a Moabite widow who had renounced her former homeland and pledged herself to Naomi’s God. Bethlehem would later become the city of David and, generations afterward, the birthplace of Jesus Christ. Ruth’s arrival therefore stands within God’s unfolding purpose to bring the Gentile woman into the line through which the Messiah would come.
