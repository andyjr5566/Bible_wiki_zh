Six Cities of Refuge

Joshua 20:1

Then the LORD said to Joshua,
The LORD continued to guide Israel through Joshua, His appointed successor to Moses, as the tribes received their inheritances in Canaan. The command that follows establishes cities of refuge according to laws already given through Moses in Numbers 35 and Deuteronomy 19. These cities protected a person who caused an unintentional death until a proper judgment could be made, preserving both justice and the sanctity of human life. Joshua’s obedience showed that Israel’s life in the land was to be ordered by God’s righteousness, not merely by military conquest or tribal custom. As Joshua led Israel into its earthly inheritance, he also foreshadowed Jesus Christ, who brings His people into the promised rest of God and provides a true refuge for all who flee to Him in faith.

Joshua 20:5

In ancient Israel, the “avenger of blood” was typically a close relative of a person who had been killed, tasked with avenging the death. This practice is rooted in the cultural and legal traditions of the time, where family honor and justice were paramount. The concept of the avenger of blood is also seen in Numbers 35:19, which outlines the role and responsibilities of this individual. The pursuit indicates a legal and familial obligation to seek justice, reflecting the societal norms of retribution.
