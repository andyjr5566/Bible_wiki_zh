Xerxes I, called Ahasuerus in Hebrew, ruled the Persian Empire from 486 to 465 BC. His reign followed that of Darius I and belonged to the period after many Jews had returned from Babylonian exile, though large Jewish communities remained dispersed throughout Persia. Esther’s account unfolds under the rule of a historically known monarch whose court, wealth, military power, and extensive administration are also reflected in ancient records. God’s covenant people lived under foreign authority, yet His purposes were not limited by imperial power or geographical distance.
