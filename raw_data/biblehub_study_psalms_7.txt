Psalm 7:1

A Shiggaion of David, which he sang to the LORD concerning the words of Cush, a Benjamite.
Shiggaion likely identifies a song marked by intense emotion, fitting David’s appeal amid accusation and danger. Cush, a Benjamite, may have been among those who repeated slander against David during Saul’s pursuit of him. Benjamin was Saul’s tribe, and opposition from a Benjamite would have been closely connected to the conflict surrounding Saul’s declining kingship. David brings the matter to the LORD rather than answering false charges with revenge.

David addresses the covenant LORD as his own God, revealing personal faith amid public hostility. Though hunted and vulnerable, he recognizes that safety does not finally rest in military strength, hiding places, or loyal companions. God Himself is the refuge of the righteous, a truth fulfilled supremely in Christ, who delivers His people from judgment and preserves them through every assault.

Israel’s sacred assemblies for worship and judgment anticipated the day when all nations will acknowledge the Lord’s sovereignty. Prophets foresaw the nations gathering to receive God’s instruction and peace, and Revelation portrays a redeemed multitude from every nation standing before His throne.

The universal judgment anticipated in the Psalms reaches its fullness in Christ, whom the Father appointed to judge the living and the dead (John 5:22; Matthew 25:31–46).

Psalm 7:10

My shield is with God,
God Himself is David’s defense against accusation, violence, and unjust judgment. A shield in ancient warfare turned aside blows that would otherwise wound or kill, making it a fitting picture of God’s active protection. The Lord had earlier promised Abram, “I am your shield” (Genesis 15:1), revealing Himself as the faithful protector of those who belong to Him. Faith likewise serves as a shield because it rests in God’s strength and promises (Ephesians 6:16). David’s confidence does not rest in military power, personal innocence, or human allies, but in the righteous God who sees every threat and judges every enemy.
