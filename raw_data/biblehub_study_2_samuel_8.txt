2 Samuel 8:17

Zadok son of Ahitub and Ahimelech son of Abiathar were priests;
Zadok descended from Eleazar, Aaron’s son, while Ahimelech belonged to the line of Ithamar through his father Abiathar. Ahimelech was likely named after his grandfather, the priest slain by Saul at Nob; Abiathar alone escaped and became David’s faithful companion during his years of exile (1 Samuel 22:20–23). The presence of both priestly lines in David’s administration reflected the Lord’s continuing provision for Israel’s worship. Zadok later anointed Solomon king and remained faithful when Abiathar supported Adonijah’s attempted seizure of the throne (1 Kings 1:39; 2:26–35). David ruled as king without claiming the priesthood, preserving the distinction between offices that finds its perfect fulfillment in Jesus Christ, the true King and eternal High Priest.

Benaiah son of Jehoiada
Benaiah was one of David’s mighty men, renowned for extraordinary courage and faithful service. He led David’s bodyguard and later replaced Joab as commander of Solomon’s army (1 Kings 2:35). His steadfast loyalty during the transfer of the kingdom to Solomon showed the value of godly courage joined to submission to God’s appointed king.

1Ki 1:8 But Zadok the priest, Benaiah son of Jehoiada,…

was over the Cherethites and Pelethites;
The Cherethites and Pelethites formed David’s elite guard, entrusted with protecting the king and carrying out sensitive royal assignments. Many were likely connected with Philistine coastal peoples, demonstrating that David’s kingdom included loyal servants from outside Israel. Their service under David anticipated the gathering of people from every nation under the reign of Christ.

and David’s sons were priestly leaders.
David’s sons held leading offices in the royal administration, serving as chief officials near the king rather than as priests in the Aaronic sanctuary order (1 Chronicles 18:17). Their positions reveal the importance of faithful leadership around the throne. Israel’s king could appoint officials, but only Jesus Christ unites perfect kingship and everlasting priesthood, ruling His people with holiness and righteousness.
