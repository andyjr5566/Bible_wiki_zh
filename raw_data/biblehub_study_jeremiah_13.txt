The queen mother occupied a place of recognized honor and influence in the Davidic court, as Bathsheba did beside Solomon (1 Kings 2:19). Judgment upon both mother and son reveals that no royal position could shield Judah from the consequences of persistent rebellion against the Lord.

The Negev had long served as Judah’s southern frontier, but its fortified cities could not withstand the judgment God had announced. Human defenses, alliances, and military strength could not reopen what the Lord had closed because Judah had persisted in idolatry and covenant rebellion.

The exile fulfilled the warnings of the Law and the prophets, demonstrating God’s faithfulness both to judge sin and to preserve a people for future restoration.
