1 Samuel 14:52

And the war with the Philistines was fierce for all the days of Saul.
The Philistines, settled along Canaan’s coastal plain, possessed military advantages, including iron weapons and organized city-states. Their pressure upon Israel threatened the tribes’ security and exposed the need for a united kingdom. God had raised Saul to begin Israel’s deliverance from the Philistines (1 Samuel 9:16), yet Saul’s repeated disobedience prevented his reign from bringing lasting victory. The conflict continued until David later broke much of Philistine power.
