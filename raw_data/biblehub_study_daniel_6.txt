The Plot against Daniel

Daniel 6:1

Now it pleased Darius
Darius the Mede assumed rule over Babylon after its fall, governing a kingdom newly brought under Medo-Persian authority. The biblical record presents him as a real ruler who exercised authority over the conquered realm. His approval of an orderly administration reveals the practical demands of governing a vast and diverse empire.

Satraps were high officials placed over districts to administer justice, preserve order, and oversee royal interests. The appointment of 120 officials provided a structure through which the king’s authority could reach every part of the kingdom. Such delegation also created the setting in which Daniel’s faithfulness and exceptional character would become visible.

The extensive territory inherited from Babylon required dependable local government. Darius could organize his kingdom, but the Lord would direct events within it for the preservation of His faithful servant and the display of His power.
