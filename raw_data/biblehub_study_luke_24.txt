The resurrection’s association with the first day helps explain why believers later gathered regularly on the first day of the week in worship and fellowship.

Their continual worship also prepared them to wait in faith for the promised Holy Spirit.

in the temple.
The temple remained the appointed center of Jewish worship in Jerusalem and the place where God’s covenant people gathered for prayer and sacrifice. Jesus had taught there, healed within its courts, confronted unbelieving leaders, and revealed Himself as the fulfillment of the Law and the Prophets. By gathering openly in the temple, the disciples testified that Jesus was not opposed to God’s revealed purposes for Israel but was their promised fulfillment. They would soon receive the Holy Spirit at Pentecost and proclaim Christ beyond Jerusalem, yet their worship began in the city where Jesus died, rose, ascended, and commanded them to wait.
